FAQ

Questions founders ask us.

Do you need access to my code or my accounts?

No. We test only the public pages of your site, the way a visitor sees them. We don't sign up, buy anything or send your forms with real data.

Who is behind Bugbeam?

Bugbeam is run by Elio Bathani, a QA tester who has tested and reported bugs on many websites. AI agents do the testing, and every report follows real QA standards.

How do I pay?

In USDT on the TRON network (TRC20). After you order, you get your own exact amount (for example 19.07 USDT). Our agents see the payment and start your test automatically.

What if a bug in the report isn't real?

Every bug is checked twice before you see it. If one still turns out to be wrong, just reply and tell us. We fix the report and our agents learn from it.

How fast do I get the report?

Within 24 hours of your payment, usually sooner. The free re-check comes 7 days later, or sooner if you reply "fixed".

I got an email from Bugbeam. Can I stop them?

Of course. Reply "STOP" to any of our emails and you won't hear from us again.
