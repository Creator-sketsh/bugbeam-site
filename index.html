FAQ

Questions founders ask us.

Do you need access to my code or my accounts?

No. We test only the public pages of your site, the way a visitor sees them. We don't sign up, buy anything or send your forms with real data.

Will the test slow down or break my site?

No. Our agents browse your pages like a handful of normal visitors — a few at a time, gently. We don't run attacks, floods or anything that could harm your site or your data.

What do you do with my data?

We keep only what we need to run your test: your site address, your email, your order and payment reference, our emails with you, and the bugs we found. We never sell it or share it with advertisers. To have it erased, reply "DELETE" to any of our emails or write to support@bugbeam.dev. Full privacy policy →
