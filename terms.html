By ordering a Bugbeam test you agree to these terms. Questions: support@bugbeam.dev.

The service

  • Quick check (free): a short check of a few public pages, sent by email.
  • Full test ($19, one time): up to 15 public pages on desktop, tablet and phone, with a PDF report within 24 hours of payment, plus one free re-check after 7 days.
  • Bugbeam Care ($69 for 3 months, paid once): a full test every month, a weekly check, an uptime check every 5 minutes with an email if your site goes down, and every report as a PDF.

What we test

We test only the public pages of the website you name. You confirm that you own the website or are allowed to order a test for it. We don't sign up, buy, submit forms with real data, or test anything behind a login unless we agree on it in writing.

Payment

You can pay in crypto through NOWPayments (USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH and other coins), by Western Union, Whish Money or OMT. Each order gets its own payment link or reference so we can match your payment. Your test starts once the payment arrives. Transfer and network fees are paid by the sender.

Refunds

  • If our agents find no real bugs on your website, we refund you in full.
  • If we can't test your website at all (for example it's offline or fully behind a login), we refund you in full.
  • If your report isn't delivered within 24 hours of your payment, we refund you in full. Just reply to tell us.
  • If a bug in your report isn't real, reply and tell us. We check it and fix the report.
  • Refunds go back the way you paid, or another way you choose, within 7 days.

Our limits

Our reports describe what we found on the pages and devices we tested, on the date of the test. We can't promise to find every bug. Our total responsibility is limited to the amount you paid for the test.

Changes

We may update these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. Orders already paid keep the terms from the day you paid.
