Bugbeam

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Bugbeam is a website testing service run by Elio Bathani. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: support@bugbeam.dev.

What we collect

  • When you order: your website address, your email address, the plan you chose, and your messages to us.
  • When you pay: the amount, the method you used and its reference (a NOWPayments payment ID and blockchain transaction ID, a Western Union tracking number, or a Whish/OMT reference). We never see or store card or bank details.
  • When we test a website: screenshots and notes about the public pages of that website.
  • When we contact a founder about a new launch: the public contact email that the website or its launch page shows.

How we use it

  • To run the tests you ordered and send you your reports.
  • To answer your emails, confirm payments and send receipts.
  • To tell founders about bugs we found on their public website, one short email at a time.

We don't sell your data and we don't share it with advertisers.

Services we use

  • Spacemail (Spaceship) to send and receive email.
  • Web3Forms to deliver the order form on this website to our inbox, with hCaptcha to block spam.
  • Payment providers to take your payment: NOWPayments (crypto), Western Union, Whish Money and OMT. Each one only gets what it needs to process your payment.
  • GitHub Pages to host this website.
  • An AI model to help check our findings and our email drafts. We only send it public page content and our own drafts.

How long we keep it

Orders, reports and receipts are kept as long as needed for your service and our records. You can ask us to delete your data at any time, unless we must keep a payment record by law.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any of our emails and we will never email you again.
  • Reply DELETE to any of our emails, or email support@bugbeam.dev, to delete the data we hold about you. You can also ask to see or correct it.

What we don't do

We only test public pages. We don't log in to your accounts, buy anything, send your forms with real data, or try to break into your website.
